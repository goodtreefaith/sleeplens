# Pressure insights

SleepLens reports observations for discussion with a clinician. It never diagnoses inadequate treatment or treatment-emergent central sleep apnea, recommends a setting, or changes settings. Device event flags and summary indices are not a sleep study. The UI must append this line to every pressure tip:

> SleepLens never changes settings. Only your clinician should change your pressure.

The research below informed implementation on 2026-11-09. The v2.2 calibration below updates the range-proximity and floor-event thresholds. The repeated-night thresholds, proximity window and leak comparison sizes are SleepLens product heuristics, not validated clinical decision limits for Aeonmed devices.

## What the sources support

- [AASM APAP practice parameters, 2007 update published 2008](https://aasm.org/resources/practiceparameters/pp_autotitrating_update.pdf) recommend clinical follow-up and review of raw data. They caution that the best percentile and monitoring duration are uncertain and results depend on the device and algorithm. A percentile alone cannot establish an appropriate prescription.
- [AASM longitudinal management guidance, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8314660/) describes reviewing adherence, leak and residual events together. Residual AHI of 5 to 15 with recurring symptoms can warrant clinician assessment. The meaning of elevated residual AHI without symptoms is uncertain. Central flags can merit review but are not themselves a diagnosis.
- [AASM PAP clinical practice guideline, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6374094/) calls for follow-up that monitors objective efficacy and usage and resolves treatment problems.
- [Philips Care Orchestrator daily details](https://sapphireprod-clientstash.6dc3-use1.007f953.hsp.philips.com/help/en/reportsguide/daily_details.htm) defines P90 as the pressure at or below which the device spent 90% of its time. [Its settings report](https://sapphireprod-clientstash.6dc3-use1.007f953.hsp.philips.com/help/en/reportsguide/compliance_summary_device_settings.htm) distinguishes this statistic from the prescribed minimum and maximum. P90 is not the observed maximum or the percentage of time at the maximum.
- [ResMed AirView report guide](https://resmedwebinars.com/hubfs/resmed-webinars/clinical-education/AirView_Report_Guide_1018991.pdf) advises reviewing event types and mask fit when unintentional leak p95 exceeds 24 L/min. [ATS adherence tracking statement, 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5447296/) documents differences in device event/leak definitions and interface thresholds, including 24 L/min for nasal and 36 L/min for full-face ResMed interfaces. Neither source validates a universal Aeonmed leak cutoff. SleepLens uses the existing contract's excess-leak guide and labels it an observation.
- [Zeineddine and Badr, CHEST, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chest.2021.01.036) describes pressure-related ventilatory instability and other causes of treatment-emergent central apnea. Its clinical definition requires a sleep-study context, central index above 5 and at least half of events central, plus exclusion of other causes. SleepLens uses a more inclusive discussion trigger, not that diagnosis.

## Rules and thresholds

All period rules require at least seven distinct dates with recorded use. Dates are deduplicated and sorted. Night rules use one night's evidence, except floor-event observations require at least three qualifying nights and only appear for periods. Every generated tip has category `pressure`, quantitative evidence and up to five most recent supporting dates. At most two pressure tips survive, in severity order. A talk observation takes precedence over a watch, info or good observation.

| Rule | Implemented threshold | Why clinicians review it and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pressure-ceiling-events` | A night is near maximum when P90 is at least maximum minus 0.25, or at least 10% of recorded mask-on seconds are within 0.5 of maximum. At least 30% of used nights must qualify, with mean residual AHI at least 5 or mean obstructive apnea index at least 2 across those nights. For one night, apply those indices to that night. | A cluster of residual events alongside use of the top of the prescribed range is relevant to efficacy review. AASM 2008 and 2021 support joint review of pressure, leak and residual events. Proximity, time-share and repeated-night thresholds are product heuristics. They do not establish that the maximum is inadequate. |
| `pressure-ceiling` | Same 30% near-maximum test; every near-maximum night has known AHI below 5 and the ceiling-events trigger is absent. A night-level info tip also requires at least 10% of recorded seconds near maximum, increased to at least 20% when AHI is below 5. | Coarse P90 one step below maximum does not establish sustained use of the top of the range. Philips distinguishes percentiles from settings. The observation does not claim that a different setting is necessary. |
| `pressure-floor-events` | At least three nights each have at least five obstructive apnea plus hypopnea events with known pressures and at least 30% of those event pressures within 0.5 of minimum. Across qualifying nights, watch requires mean obstructive plus hypopnea index at least 3/hour or mean AHI at least 5. Otherwise info requires mean obstructive plus hypopnea index at least 1/hour; lower burden is suppressed. No night-level tip. | Event timing and pressure can help a clinician understand the device response. Small event counts can produce large percentages, so the share alone does not establish a meaningful burden. Ramp events count only within the same proximity window. Info wording: "Some breathing events happened at the lowest pressure, often early in the night. Mention this at your next review." These thresholds are product heuristics. |
| `pressure-low-use` | A night is near minimum when P90 is at most minimum plus 0.25, or at least 50% of recorded mask-on seconds are within 0.5 of minimum. At least 70% of used nights must qualify; every used night has known AHI below 5 and obstructive apnea plus hypopnea rate below 2/hour of use. | A range used mainly near its minimum can be mentioned at routine review. The proximity, time-share and repeated-night thresholds are product heuristics, not evidence that the prescription is excessive. The event guard prevents reassurance when other nights have substantial residual events. Partial OA/HI summaries do not establish few events. If both indices are absent, the raw rate and known AHI must both be below 2. |
| `central-with-pressure` | Any night with machine central apnea index above 5, or more than 50% of counted events central with AHI above 5. | The CHEST review and AASM 2021 explain why central flags merit clinician review. This OR condition intentionally retains the contract's inclusive trigger. It does not diagnose pressure causation or central apnea. |
| `leak-at-high-pressure` | Within a night, mean excess leak in the upper third of the prescribed pressure range is above 24 L/min and at least 10 L/min above the bottom third, with at least 60 valid seconds in each group. Alternatively, period mean excess-leak p95 on upper-third P90 nights meets the same difference, or mean large-leak share is above 5% and at least 5 percentage points above bottom-third P90 nights. | ResMed and ATS support checking leak and mask fit; AASM advises resolving interface problems before interpreting residual events. Outer thirds, 60 seconds, differences of 10 L/min and 5 percentage points are product definitions of the contract's qualitative phrase 'clearly higher'. They identify association, not cause. |
| `pressure-ok` | No other pressure trigger; all used nights have AHI below 5, P90 within the prescribed range, do not meet the calibrated near-maximum definition, have excess-leak p95 at most 24 L/min and large-leak share at most 5%. | These are reassuring product guides, interpreted together with regular clinical follow-up as recommended by AASM. Missing AHI or leak prevents this reassurance. Symptoms and clinician judgment still matter. |

Boundary observations apply only to valid Auto CPAP or AUTOS ranges with positive minimum and maximum greater than minimum. CPAP and bilevel modes do not receive APAP range observations. A recorded prescription change within the night suppresses these observations because one snapshot cannot describe both prescriptions. Central and within-night leak observations still report their evidence.

## Pressure facts and period summary

`night_pressure_facts` keeps settings, coarse summary P90 and observed maximum separate. Recorded 1 Hz pressure samples supply observed maximum and the percentage within 0.5 of each limit. Finite positive samples represent recorded mask-on seconds. Null gaps, invalid samples and missing seconds are excluded from this denominator. Summary usage can differ from recorded sample duration. The share is unavailable without samples, rather than inferred from P90. The summary maximum is a fallback when the 1 Hz series is absent.

Obstructive apnea, hypopnea and central event pressure is decoded from the raw event value multiplied by 0.5. Zero raw values are treated as missing. The normalized optional event-pressure field is not trusted in place of this decode. The minimum share uses only obstructive apnea and hypopnea events with a known minimum setting, pooled across nights; central events do not enter it. Floor proximity is an absolute difference of at most 0.5, including any ramp events in that window.

`PressureSummary` uses the most recent available prescription in the range, including a fixed-pressure snapshot. Each night's proximity counts use its own prescription and the calibrated P90-or-time-share definitions above. Average P90 and average time-at-maximum share are unweighted per-night means over available values. A missing minimum excludes that night's events from the event-share denominator. No comparable events gives `eventsAtMinPct: null`.

Compact facts APIs let the store decode each nightly series, retain its facts and discard the series before reading the next night. This avoids holding a year's 1 Hz data in memory. Period calculations do not reread a card or access the network.

Generated text deliberately describes the range without numeric pressure values, setting verbs or pressure targets. Synthetic tests cover every rule, threshold boundaries, unavailable data, evidence dates and forbidden wording. The insights are unvalidated product heuristics and need clinical evaluation before any claim about treatment outcomes.

## Implementation verification, 2026-11-09

The backend implementation includes all seven rules, thirteen core pressure tests and a store round-trip test covering each rule over seven synthetic nights. The score uses REM alone, excludes uncertain minutes from estimated sleep and omits its ten-point component above 40% uncertain mask-on time. Database schema 4 refreshes stored scores from schemas 1, 2 and 3. Stage blob version 2 stores Uncertain as byte 5 and reads legacy metadata without the new fields. Estimator versions below 3 remain eligible for card rereading.

The final workspace test run passed 100 tests; the two real-card tests compiled and remained ignored. Workspace Clippy passed with warnings denied. No Git writes or reads under `/Volumes` were performed. This verification uses synthetic data and does not establish clinical accuracy.

Scoped formatting passed for all files owned by this backend task. The final `cargo fmt --all --check` reported one formatting difference in the concurrently edited `crates/sleeplens-core/tests/sleep_stages.rs`, outside this task's ownership; that file was left untouched.

## v2.2 calibration, 2026-11-09

Faith's reported AS100 observations on 2026-11-09 prompted these changes. P90 of 7.5 against maximum 8 with 6% of recorded time near maximum no longer counts as near maximum. Low event burden suppresses floor-event observations even when a large fraction occurs near minimum. These are product-noise controls, not validated clinical cutoffs. Regression coverage uses synthetic data only. Calibration work reads no real cards and performs no Git writes.

Calibration verification passed with `/opt/homebrew/opt/rustup/bin` first on `PATH`: `cargo test --workspace` (112 passed, two real-card tests ignored), `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, and `cargo fmt --all --check`. The 19 core pressure tests include both floor-event wording variants in the forbidden-wording check. Synthetic store tests verify the calibrated rules after persistence and period aggregation. These results supersede the earlier formatting gap above.
